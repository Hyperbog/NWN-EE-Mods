# M8 — Stabilized wall contact

M7 cloud release is published. User reports no clipping during the test, but the camera jumps position to avoid clips. The supplied 2026-11-25 cx.log shows M6 fallback abort after 266 ms and active Driving mode 2; it does not contain per-frame M7 edge-correction values or a release hash. The M7 source independently reveals an immediate per-frame worst-ray displacement, up to 0.24 m; this is a plausible source of discontinuity, not a measured attribution of every jump.

M8 retains M7's exact raw collision solver, but carries a world-space edge offset across frames. It approaches the raw correction at 1.8 m/s and releases at 0.8 m/s when validated, with the original margin acting as a buffer. The actual near rectangle plus 5 mm skin is tested at each proposed intermediate, as are anchor-to-candidate and solved-to-candidate segments. Up to three intermediate candidates are considered if the first fails; otherwise the original M7 response is preserved. No blending of mouse yaw/pitch or ordinary eye position. The earlier thirteen-ray sweep is unchanged. History resets on lost guard, scene/player change, large anchor movement or frame gaps; open space with no offset adds no validation casts. Diagnostic counters distinguish smoothed frames and hard fallbacks. This is a bounded sampled approximation: unavoidable hard corrections and original sweep jumps may remain, and additional casts near walls have a performance cost. Rates are initial tuning choices; runtime validation is pending.

Cloud build plan is advanced to M8. M7 release stays available. The installer takes the exact published commit tag and preserves the previously installed DLL set. No local compilation.
